Quantum-Enhanced Build v3.3.0
2026-09-20

v3.3 builds on v3.2's real quantum-state analysis, calibrated India map, and honest-labelling
guarantees, and adds: a unified Data Quality Score, richer sensor anomaly detection (including
stuck/repeated-reading detection), a structured INFO/WATCH/WARNING/CRITICAL alert engine with
What/Why/Check/Action explanations, configurable crop profiles (config/crops/*.json), time-series
field history with an honest "not enough historical data" state, a farm-events timeline, a
weather-provider abstraction that degrades gracefully instead of crashing, an experimental
quantum circuit-sensitivity sweep (explicitly not validated feature importance), and a one-click,
clearly-labelled Demo Mode with six scenarios that never writes to real farmer data.

The package still does not fabricate agricultural accuracy. Real vision weights, Qiskit ML
artifacts, and expert-labelled field validation remain explicit external release gates. See
CHANGELOG_v3.3.md, docs/ARCHITECTURE_V3.3.md, SECURITY.md, TESTING.md and DEMO_GUIDE.md for detail
on exactly what changed, what was verified, and what remains future work.
